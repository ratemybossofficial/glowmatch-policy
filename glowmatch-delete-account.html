📋 How to Delete Your Account

To delete your GlowMatch account and all associated data, follow these steps:

  1. Open the app and navigate to Home from the bottom tab bar.
  2. Tap the profile icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the profile screen and tap "계정 삭제" (Delete Account).
  4. Confirm the prompt — your account and data will be deleted immediately.

📧 Request via Email

If you cannot access the app, you may request deletion by email. Requests are processed within 7 business days.
